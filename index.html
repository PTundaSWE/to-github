  • Fly Appearance: Black/brown clusters on pumpkin fruits and leaves.
  • Damage Pattern: Holes or tunnels inside fruits, curling/yellowing leaves, and rotting stems.
  • Stage of Attack: Flies visible on leaves, flowers, and fruit surfaces.

Step-by-Step Treatment Plan

  1. Remove and Destroy Infected Fruits: Collect all infested fruits and bury them 2 feet deep or burn them. Avoid composting to prevent re-infestation.
  2. Mechanical and Cultural Control: Clean garden areas, remove weeds, and use mulch to prevent fly breeding.
  3. Organic / Natural Control Options:
    • Use neem oil spray to repel flies and other pests.
    • Apply garlic-chili spray for broad pest control.

Neem Solution

  • Mix 100 mL neem oil with 10 L water + drops of soap.
  • Spray every 3–4 days for 2 weeks on leaves & fruits.
  • Repels fruit flies, aphids, and whiteflies effectively.

Garlic-Chili Spray

  • Blend 10 garlic cloves + 5 chilies + 1 L water, let sit overnight.
  • Strain and dilute in 5 L water.
  • Spray every 3–5 days for best protection.

Trap Control for Fruit Flies

Set up homemade bait traps using yeast and sugar solution:

  1. Mix 1 tbsp sugar + 1 tbsp yeast + 500 mL water.
  2. Pour into cut plastic bottles and hang 1 m above ground.
  3. Replace solution every 5 days.

Prevention for Next Season

  1. Deep plow soil after harvest to expose fly pupae.
  2. Intercrop with basil, marigold, or garlic to repel flies.
  3. Use neem-treated seedlings for early-stage pest prevention.
